Privacy Policy

SlideX · Willburs Software Solutions

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Willburs Software Solutions ("we", "us", "our") built the SlideX app as a free-to-play game with optional in-app purchases. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, third-party services integrated in the app may collect data as described below.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

SlideX uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • IP address (used to determine approximate location)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data

If you do not grant ATT permission, only non-personalized ads will be shown. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit 2. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to all purchase transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Local Data Storage

SlideX stores game progress, settings, and preferences locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

  • Game progress (completed levels, stars, world unlocks)
  • Lives and recovery timestamps
  • Language and audio preferences
  • Hero name (entered by you)

5. Push Notifications

SlideX may send local push notifications to remind you when your lives have recovered or to maintain your daily streak. These notifications are scheduled on-device and do not involve any server-side tracking. You can disable them at any time in your iPhone Settings.

6. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, the app will request permission to track your activity for advertising purposes (ATT prompt). You can choose to allow or deny this request. Your choice does not affect gameplay.

7. Children's Privacy

SlideX does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@willburs.com
